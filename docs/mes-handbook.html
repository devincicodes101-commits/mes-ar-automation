00

What the business actually does

context

MES Group owns worker dormitories in Singapore. They rent bed-space to construction and marine companies, who house their migrant workers there. So the customer is an employer, not a worker — which is why every account in the data is a company, and why chasing payment means emailing a finance department.

Many pay late. Today the CSD team exports spreadsheets from NetSuite, reads them by eye, types reminder emails, phones the late payers, and writes notes like Payment by 29.05.26 into a column. This system replaces that.

This is the question everything else depends on, so plainly: one file every cycle, and a second file only when it changes.

So: one sheet or two?

One sheet, from one file, each cycle. The AR report is a single-sheet workbook and that is the only thing that has to be re-uploaded. The contact list is a second file uploaded once and reused; the officer can also type a missing address straight into the app, which is saved and survives the next AR upload.

The system tells the two apart by looking inside them — the contact list is the one with an Email Address column — so they can be dropped in any order.

Why this matters more than it looks

An invoice 30 days past due lands in the 30 days bucket, and one 15 days past due is still Current. Both the earlier SOP and our own proposal described a "30-day trigger line", which would have put thousands of invoices in the wrong column. The system computes the bucket itself and compares it to the one MES print, reporting any disagreement rather than inheriting it.

06

Rule · what a charge is for

You would expect outstanding − deposit held: what MES loses if the tenant walks away. But one row shows $418.64 overdue against $16,640 of deposit and a Risk Exposure of −$3,162.60. We tested roughly a thousand combinations of every column pair and triple. None matched.

This is not a hard inference. It is an impossible one: the inputs in the sample don't agree with each other, so no formula could reproduce the output.
