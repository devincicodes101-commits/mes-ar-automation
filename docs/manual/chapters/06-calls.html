Call List and payment promises

MES chases with a letter and then a phone call. The Call List says who to phone today and why, and records what came of each call. When a tenant agrees to pay, the promise is kept on the Payment promises tab until the date comes.

Who to phone

A tenant joins the list once their first reminder date has passed and they still owe. They are due a first call after the first reminder, and a second call after the final notice. Worst cases come first.

A tenant billed on several dates is on the list once. The call is about everything they owe and follows their latest letter.

Logging a call

  1. Phone the tenant. Their account details are at the top of the call window, so you do not need to look anything up.
  2. Press Log this call on their row.
  3. Check which reminder the call is about. It is filled in for you from the list.
  4. Type who you spoke to, for example "Serene, accounts".
  5. Choose what happened. Nothing is chosen for you.
  6. If they agreed to pay, check the amount, which starts as what they have overdue, and type the date they said they would pay by. The date cannot be in the past.
  7. Set a date to check back if you want a reminder to call again, and add any note worth keeping.
  8. Press Save this call.

The notice after saving says where the call went: "Recorded in the activity log and the NetSuite export", or, for a promise, "added to Payment Promises".

Calls already logged

Below the list of who to phone, the Calls logged box keeps every call, newest first.

Reading a logged call

View the call shows every answer from the call form, read only, including the date to check back, which is not shown in the list.

Payment promises

The Payment promises tab lists every promise to pay, whether it came from a call or from a tenant replying to a reminder.

Recording a promise by hand

  1. Press Record a promise.
  2. Find the tenant by typing part of their name or code, and choose them.
  3. Type the amount and the date they promised to pay by.
  4. Choose where it came from: a reply to a reminder, or a phone call.
  5. Press Record it.

What happens in the background

  • The list follows the letters. The first call is due once the first reminder date has passed. The second call is due once the final notice has really gone. For a tenant with no email address, the second call waits for the first call instead.
  • A promise takes the tenant off the list until its date, and holds their letters too. They stay visible under Promised to pay so nobody rings them by mistake.
  • A promise that passes unpaid moves to "Date passed, still unpaid" and counts towards Value at risk. The tenant comes back onto the list to phone.
  • A promise from a tenant who has since paid, meaning they are gone from a newer report or owe nothing, is marked "Paid, no longer owing" and is not counted as money promised.
  • Every call and promise is written to the Activity Log and goes into the NetSuite file on Reports and Export.
  • A Relationship Manager sees and logs calls for their own tenants only.
