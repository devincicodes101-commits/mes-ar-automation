Late Payment Fees

MES charges a late payment fee of S$100, before GST, on bills left unpaid more than 14 days past their due date. The 9am run raises the fees on the 16th of each month. This screen shows who is charged and why, gives you the listing for the AR team, and lets you raise the fees yourself if the run could not.

The screen

Who is charged, and why

Each row in "Fees that would be raised this month" is one tenant.

Below the list, two more boxes may appear.

  • Left out: on GIRO. Tenants who have a Rejected GIRO Fee in the report. A bounced GIRO deduction is the bank's failure, not theirs, so they are not charged.
  • Settled: these tenants have since paid. Tenants charged earlier who no longer owe. Press Show to list them.

Raising the fees yourself

Normally there is nothing to do: the 9am run raises the fees on the 16th. Raise them here only if the run did not, for example if the Activity Log shows the 16th was missed.

  1. Check the list. Use the search box to find a tenant you are unsure about.
  2. Press Review and raise. A window says how many fee notices will be raised and the total, and repeats the rule.
  3. Press Raise the fees. The notice confirms how many were raised.
Good to know

A tenant can be charged only once a month. If some fees were already raised this month, by the 9am run or by someone else, the screen says how many and leaves them out, so pressing Raise the fees twice never charges anyone twice.

Changing the fee for one run

Change for this run lets you charge a different fixed amount or a percentage of what is overdue, or charge only tenants who owe more than a set amount. The change applies to fees raised from this screen, this time. It does not change the 9am run. To change when the 9am run charges, use Settings, Fees and reports (section 9.7).

What happens in the background

  • The rule. On the 16th, a tenant is charged when they have a bill more than 14 days past its due date that is still unpaid. Settings can count from the billing date instead.
  • Invoice dates decide, not the aging columns. MES counts anything up to 15 days past due as Current. On the 16th the month that has just gone unpaid is still in Current, so the system looks at each bill's own dates rather than the columns.
  • Once per tenant per month. However many bills are late, the fee is S$100 once.
  • GIRO tenants are left out, and tenants in credit are never charged.
  • Where a fee goes. A raised fee is recorded against the tenant and appears on their page and in the Activity Log. The system does not change NetSuite itself: the AR team bills the fees there from the downloaded listing.
