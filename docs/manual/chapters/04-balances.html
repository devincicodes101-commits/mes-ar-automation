Outstanding Balances

Outstanding Balances is the first screen after signing in. It lists every tenant in the report in use, with what they owe and how late it is, most overdue first. Use it to answer "how much is outstanding and who owes it", and to open any tenant's own page.

The screen

The list shows 25 tenants at a time. Use Previous and Next at the bottom to move through it.

Money by age

Press Aging columns to see each tenant's money split into Current, 30 days, 60 days, 90 days and More than 90 days. Click any column heading to sort by it, and click again to reverse the order. Everything to the right of the thicker line, 30 days and older, needs chasing.

Adding an email address

A tenant with no email address cannot be sent reminders. In the aging columns view their row shows No email, add one.

  1. Press No email, add one on the tenant's row.
  2. Type the address. For more than one, separate them with commas.
  3. Press Enter. The notice "Email saved" confirms it, and the tenant gets their reminders from now on.

To add many addresses at once, use the contact list on Upload Reports instead. An address typed in here is kept when a new contact list or report is uploaded.

Charge types and billing runs

At the bottom of the screen, Show charge types and billing runs opens two more tables.

One tenant's page

Click a tenant's name anywhere in the system to open their page. It shows everything about that tenant in one place.

Back to Outstanding Balances at the top returns you to the screen you came from.

What happens in the background

  • Every figure comes from the report in use. The date under Tenants, "Report of 15 Sep 2026" for example, is that report's own date, not today's.
  • Overdue follows MES's aging. Money is overdue once it reaches the 30 days column. Current covers anything up to 15 days past its due date.
  • A tenant in credit is never chased. They get no letters and no late fee.
  • A Relationship Manager sees only their own tenants on this screen, and the totals at the top are for those tenants only.
