Signing in, the menu and who can do what

Everyone at MES who uses the system has their own login. What you see in the menu depends on your role, so two people can open the system and see different screens. This chapter covers signing in, finding your way around, and what each role is allowed to do.

Signing in and out

Open mes-ar-automation.vercel.app in any browser. It works the same in Chrome, Edge and Safari, on a computer or a phone.

If you have forgotten your password, ask an administrator. Passwords are never sent by email.

To sign out, press the arrow button at the bottom of the menu, next to your name. Sign out when you finish on a shared computer.

The menu

The menu on the left is grouped the way the month runs. The cycle holds the screens you use every month, Review holds the reports, and Admin holds the settings.

Hover over a menu item to see a one-line description of the screen. A tenant's name anywhere in the system is a link to that tenant's own page.

The roles

There are five roles. An administrator gives each person one when creating their login (chapter 10).

RoleWhat they doMenu they see
Super AdminEverything, including managing other administrators.Everything
AdminThe full monthly cycle: upload, send, call, raise fees, export. Changes settings and manages logins, but not a Super Admin's.Everything
CSD OfficerThe daily collections work: upload, send, call, record promises, raise fees, reports. Cannot change settings or manage logins.Everything except Settings and User Management
Relationship ManagerOnly the tenants assigned to them. They can log a call and record a promise, and nothing else. They never see tenant email addresses.Outstanding Balances and Call List, showing their own tenants only
ManagementTotals and reports across every property. Read only.Outstanding Balances, Reports and Export, Activity Log

The full list of what each role can do is on User Management, under the tab "What each role can do".

Good to know

The roles are enforced by the system itself, not only by hiding menu items. A Relationship Manager who types the address of another screen is refused, and the system checks every tenant they ask for is one of their own.

Using it on a phone

Every screen works on a phone. The menu folds away behind the button at the top left, and tables become lists you scroll down.
