Glossary and common questions

Glossary

TermMeaning
9am runThe work the system does on its own every morning at 9am Singapore time: letters due that day, and the late fees on the 16th.
Ad hocA bill dated on any day other than MES's regular billing day, the 15th.
AR reportThe accounts receivable export from NetSuite that everything is worked out from.
AgingMoney grouped by how old it is: Current, 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, More than 90 days.
Billing dateThe date a bill was raised, from the Date column of the AR report. Each one starts its own reminder clock.
By handA letter for a tenant with no email address, posted from Outlook by an officer.
ChaseableMoney more than 15 days past its due date.
Due dateWhen payment was due: from the report, or 14 days after billing.
Final noticeThe second letter, 37 days after billing as standard.
First reminderThe first letter, 23 days after billing as standard.
GIRO tenantA tenant who pays by GIRO, recognised by a Rejected GIRO Fee line in the report. Never charged the late fee.
Held backA letter written but not sent, for example in test mode. It is recorded with the reason.
In creditA tenant who has paid more than they owe. Never chased.
Late payment feeS$100 before GST, raised on the 16th for bills more than 14 days past due.
OverdueMoney 30 days old or more.
Promise to payA tenant's commitment to pay an amount by a date. Holds their letters until then.
Report in useThe AR report every screen and the 9am run are reading.
Words in double bracesParts of a letter filled in on the day it goes: {{company}}, {{code}}, {{property}}, {{amount}}, {{overdue}}, {{today}} and {{dueBy}}.

Common questions

A tenant says they never got their reminder.

Open Reminder Emails, Sent, and search for the tenant. If the letter is there, open it to see the address it went to and when. If it says "not sent", the Activity Log, 9am runs tab has the reason. If there is no letter, check All tenants: the bill may not have reached its day yet, or the tenant may have a promise or no email address.

Nothing went out this morning.

Open Settings, Mailbox. The line at the top says why: sending is off, the mailbox needs reconnecting, or no mailbox is connected. Then look at Needs attention on the Activity Log, which has a button to send the missed letters once the cause is fixed.

A tenant has paid but is still being chased.

The system knows a tenant has paid only from a newer AR report. Upload the current report from NetSuite and they drop off the letters and the Call List.

A tenant was charged a late fee they should not have been.

Check Late Payment Fees and the tenant's page for the bills the fee was for. Fees are billed in NetSuite from the listing, so correct it there. If the cause is a GIRO tenant without a Rejected GIRO Fee line, tell the system administrator.

The numbers look wrong after an upload.

Check Report in use on Upload Reports is the file you meant, and its date. If not, upload the right file. It replaces the wrong one and nothing else is lost.

Someone has forgotten their password.

An Admin opens User Management, presses Reset password on their row, and gives them the new password (section 10.4).

Someone has left MES.

Switch their login off on User Management. If their mailbox is the active one, connect another mailbox and press Make active on it first, then remove theirs.

Getting help

When reporting a problem, say what you were doing, which screen, the tenant if there was one, the time, and what you saw. A screenshot helps. Most problems can be traced from the Activity Log, so the time matters most.
