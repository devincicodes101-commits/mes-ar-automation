Reports and Export

Reports and Export is where information leaves the system: the file that takes calls, letters and promises back into NetSuite, the regular reports for CSD, management and each relationship manager, and any report emailed with its workbook attached.

The screen

The file for NetSuite

Everything the team has done, one row per action: each email that was delivered, each call and each promise. Load it back into NetSuite so the tenant's record there shows the chase.

  1. Press Preview the export.
  2. Read the rows: date, tenant, what kind of action, what it was, and any amount.
  3. Press Download as CSV and import the file into NetSuite.

Letters that were written but never delivered are left out of the file, and the screen says how many. Late fees are not in this file. The AR team bills them in NetSuite from the listing on Late Payment Fees.

Regular reports

The system does not send these by itself. On the day, press Generate now to download the spreadsheet, or email it from Email a report.

Each manager's sheet

The Relationship manager balances box lists each manager with their number of tenants, what they owe and how much is overdue. Open the sheet shows that manager's tenants in MES's own layout, dormitory by dormitory.

Tenants with no manager in the report are grouped under "No manager assigned". "NetSuite system account" is NetSuite's own internal account, not a person.

Emailing a report

  1. Choose the report.
  2. Tick who it goes to.
  3. Press View the report and check it.
  4. Press Send with the workbook. The notice says who it went to.

What happens in the background

  • Reports use the report in use. Every figure is from the AR report on Upload Reports, laid out by dormitory and then by charge type, the order MES asked for.
  • What you preview is what is sent. The workbook attached to an email is built by the same step as the download.
  • Recipients' addresses come from Settings, Report recipients. The managers listed are the ones named in the report, so a new manager appears there on their own.
  • Every export and every report sent is recorded in the Activity Log.
  • Nothing goes into NetSuite automatically. The file only reaches NetSuite when someone imports it.
