User Management

User Management is where an administrator gives people a login, sets their role, and switches a login off when someone leaves. Only an Admin or Super Admin can open it.

The screen

Adding a login

  1. Press Add a login.
  2. Type the person's full name and their email address. The email address is their username.
  3. Choose their role. The line below the role says what it allows.
  4. For a Relationship Manager, choose whose tenants they see. The list is the managers named in the AR report.
  5. A first password is filled in for you. You can type your own instead, of at least 10 characters.
  6. Press Create the login.
  7. Give the person their email address and first password yourself. Nothing is emailed to them.

Changing a role

Choose the new role in the drop-down on the person's row. The change is saved at once, and the notice says what they are now. For a Relationship Manager, a second drop-down chooses whose tenants they see.

Two limits apply. Nobody can change their own role, and an Admin cannot change, or switch off, a Super Admin.

Resetting a password

When someone has forgotten their password, press Reset password on their row. A new password is filled in for you; press New for another, or type your own of at least 10 characters. Press Set the new password. Their old password stops working at once. Give them the new one yourself, because nothing is emailed. An Admin can reset anyone's password except a Super Admin's, and every reset is recorded in the Activity Log as "Reset a password".

Switching a login off

When someone leaves, press Switch off on their row. They can no longer sign in, and the row is marked "Switched off". Their name stays on everything they did, so the history is still readable. Press Switch on to let them back in.

Good to know

Logins are never deleted, only switched off. That way the Activity Log can always say who did what.

What happens in the background

  • Passwords are kept by the sign-in service, never by the system itself, and are never emailed.
  • A role is checked on every action, not only on what the menu shows.
  • A Relationship Manager's tenants are the ones the AR report names them as manager for. If the report moves a tenant to another manager, the tenant moves with it.
  • Every login added, changed or switched off is recorded in the Activity Log with who did it.
