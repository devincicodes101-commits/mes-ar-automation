What the system does

MES AR Automation chases unpaid rent and charges for MES Group. You upload the accounts receivable report from NetSuite, and the system works out who owes what and since when. It sends each tenant the right reminder on the right day, tells you who to phone, and works out the late payment fee. Most of this happens on its own, every morning at 9am Singapore time.

The monthly cycle in one picture

Every bill has its own clock. The clock starts on the bill's billing date, which is the Date column of the AR report. The picture follows one bill raised on 15 August. A bill raised on any other day goes through the same steps, counted from its own date.

The rules it follows

These rules hold on every screen and in the 9am run. Chapter 12 explains each one in more detail.

  • Each billing date is chased on its own. A tenant billed on 15 August and again on 1 September gets letters for both. Each letter asks for that date's total and does not list the invoices.
  • A letter goes on its day. If the 9am run could not send on day 23, for example because no mailbox was connected, the letter is not sent late. Settings can allow a few days' grace.
  • A tenant missing from a newer report has paid. They stop getting letters, leave the Call List, and any promise they made is marked "Paid, no longer owing".
  • A promise to pay holds the letters. While a promise date has not passed, the tenant gets no reminder and is not on the list to phone.
  • Ad hoc bills are bills dated on any day other than the 15th, MES's regular billing day. They are chased the same way and marked Ad hoc so you can tell them apart.
  • An old report still runs. If the newest report is more than 16 days old, the 9am run carries on and the Activity Log shows a warning.
  • Nothing reaches a tenant unless sending is switched on and a mailbox is connected. Settings, Mailbox shows which is the case.
Good to know

The system never changes NetSuite and never takes money from a bank account. Letters, calls and promises go back into NetSuite through the file on Reports and Export, and late fees through the listing on Late Payment Fees.
