Blockers

What is stopping work, in order

Two sit with MES. Two are ours and are already being fixed.

The invoice amounts are blank blocker 1 · with MES

Every one of the 169 invoice lines has an empty amount column, in both the file sent last week and the one sent this week. Oddly, the four credit note rows in the same tab do carry amounts, which suggests a filter left on during export rather than a missing column.

Without it there is no charge breakdown, no revenue split and no late fee calculation. The dormitory tabs still give us totals, so work continues, but this is the one item that cannot be worked around.

needs: a re-export with amounts

Two report columns have no formula blocker 2 · with MES

The manager report mockup has Overdue Total and Risk Exposure columns. Checked against every row, neither follows a rule we can derive, and two of the Risk Exposure figures are negative.

The mockup is illustrative, so invented numbers are expected. But it means the formulas cannot be reverse engineered and have to be stated.

needs: the calculation, in one sentence each

One record per month, not per report blocker 3 · ours

Our database allows one record per company per month. That was correct when we believed the report was uploaded once a month. MES uploads on the 4th, the 7th and the 16th — all in August.

So the first upload succeeds and the second is rejected. Everything else is built on top of this, so it is being fixed first.

ours · fix in progress

1FM charges identified the wrong way blocker 4 · ours

MES identify a 1FM charge by its invoice number. We built it by searching the description text. An invoice carries several lines and usually only one of them mentions 1FM, so the VAT and sick bay lines on the same invoice are being missed.

We are currently catching 17 lines out of 45. Small fix, and their file told us exactly how to do it.

ours · fix in progress
Open questions

What we need from MES

Each of these is a sentence to answer, not a piece of work. Together they unblock the reporting and the charge breakdown.

  1. Why is the Open Balance column empty on all 169 invoice rows?

    The four credit note rows in the same tab do have amounts, so it looks like a filter rather than a missing column. Knowing which is faster than a blind re-export.

  2. How are Overdue Total and Risk Exposure calculated?

    Neither follows a rule we can see in the mockup, and two Risk Exposure figures are negative.

  3. Harry’s and Ray’s mockups have different columns. Which one is the report to build?

    Ray’s adds Overdue Total, Update, Security Deposit and Risk Exposure. Harry’s has none of them.

  4. In the list SD / PF / 1FM / LP / SD / RM, which SD is Security Deposit and which is Stamp Duty?

    The abbreviation is used twice, in the workflow note and again in the tab names.

  5. Should 1FM credit notes count as 1FM?

    1FM invoices are numbered JP1FM. Two 1FM credit notes are numbered JP1CN, so a rule based on the invoice prefix alone would miss them.

  6. Is credit measured from the billing date or from the 1st of the month it covers?

    The workflow calls the 1st the 14-day point, but it is 17 days after a 15 July bill. Counting from the 1st makes both figures line up.

  7. Should reminder letters use different bank details per dormitory?

    The letters we have quote MES & JPD Housing Pte Ltd, which is the JPD1 company. BSD and LEO sit under different entities.

  8. Six companies appear on a dormitory tab but not on the contact list. Missing, or deliberately excluded?

    Those six cannot be sent a reminder at all until an address exists for them.

  9. The revenue categories list has not arrived yet.

    We have built our own from 345 real descriptions and it classifies all of them, but it is our reading of their categories rather than their own list.
