Example Case: Complete AR Lifecycle
ABC Construction Pte Ltd
Tenant at Westlite Dormitory | Account #DRM-2024-0847
Monthly Rent: SGD 12,500
GIRO Status: Enrolled
Segment: Construction
15th May

SOA Generated & Sent

System auto-generates Statement of Account for SGD 12,500 and emails to ABC Construction's finance contact.

NetSuite → Auto-Email
20th May

GIRO Collection Failed

DBS attempts GIRO deduction. Transaction fails with reason: "REFER PAYING PARTY" (insufficient funds).

DBS Bank
21st May

Failed GIRO Auto-Processed

Bot parses DBS Bulk Collection Report → Matches ABC Construction in NetSuite → Flags account as "Payment Issue" → Adds to follow-up queue.

DBS Parser → NetSuite API
30th May

Second SOA Sent

System sends second SOA reminder showing SGD 12,500 outstanding.

NetSuite → Auto-Email
1st June

AR Aging Trigger

Daily aging report detects ABC Construction has crossed 30-day threshold. Account queued for reminder sequence.

NetSuite API → Queue
7th June

First Reminder Email Sent

Automated first reminder sent. Template selected based on: GIRO-enrolled + Westlite property. Email logged to customer profile.

Email Engine → NetSuite Log
14th June

Phone Call Executed

AI voice agent calls ABC Construction. Customer confirms payment will be made by 20th June. Promise-to-pay logged automatically.

AI Voice → NetSuite Log
16th June

Late Fee Calculated

System calculates admin fee of SGD 125 (1%). Penalty invoice generated. New balance: SGD 12,625. Notice sent to customer.

Fee Engine → NetSuite Invoice
20th June

Payment Received

ABC Construction pays SGD 12,625 via bank transfer. Payment matched and applied. Account cleared. Activity logged.

NetSuite Payment → Log
